The Story

Design, dev and marketing — all in one person.

Most people specialise in one thing. I never could. I started in event technology — building registration platforms, apps and websites for large-scale conferences and corporate events across Australia and internationally. That meant working fast, to brief, and always with a clear business outcome in mind.

At Jomablue, I worked as Digital Marketing Coordinator, managing their WordPress website, running campaigns across LinkedIn and Google Ads, and maintaining Salesforce CRM and Mailchimp — while contributing to award-winning work recognised at the Event Technology Awards in London.

Now I'm channelling all of that into Stackly — a platform that makes professional web infrastructure, automation and marketing systems accessible to small businesses who've been priced out by agencies.
