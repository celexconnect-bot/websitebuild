FAQ

Common questions

Q Does it work with my form plugin?

FormFlow is compatible with the four most popular WordPress form plugins: WPForms, Gravity Forms, Fluent Forms, and Contact Form 7. If you're using one of those, you're good to go. Support for additional form plugins is on the roadmap — reach out if yours isn't listed and we'll let you know when it's coming.

Q Do I need coding skills?

None at all. FormFlow was specifically designed for non-technical business owners. Install it from the WordPress.org plugin directory, connect your form to a destination using a simple dropdown menu, and you're done. No API keys to hunt down, no webhooks to configure by hand, no developer required.

Q What CRMs does it connect to?

FormFlow Pro currently connects directly to FluentCRM and ActiveCampaign, with Mailchimp and ConvertKit handled as email marketing integrations. For any CRM that isn't natively supported yet — including HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho or others — FormFlow's Zapier and Make webhook output lets you connect to virtually any platform. More native CRM integrations are being added with each Pro update.

Q What happens after my Pro licence expires?

Your site and all existing connections keep working exactly as they are — FormFlow never stops functioning when a licence expires. You'll lose access to updates and priority support until you renew, but nothing breaks. Renewing is $49/yr and keeps you on the latest version with all new integrations included.
